# Acme ML Platform: GPU Quota Guide

Each research team receives a base quota of 64 GPU-hours per week on H200 nodes. Teams with an approved project charter receive an additional 128 GPU-hours per week.

Unused quota does not roll over. Quota resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Jobs requesting more than 8 GPUs must use the multi-node queue and require a reservation at least 48 hours in advance.

The internal chargeback rate is 3.20 USD per H200 GPU-hour.
