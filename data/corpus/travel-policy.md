# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy (FY2026)

Employees may book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires VP approval.

The daily meal allowance is 75 USD in domestic cities and 95 USD in international cities. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Hotel stays are capped at 250 USD per night in tier-1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) and 180 USD per night elsewhere.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date. Reports submitted after 60 days are rejected.
