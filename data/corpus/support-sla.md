# Acme Enterprise Support SLA

Severity 1 (production down): first response within 1 hour, 24x7.
Severity 2 (degraded): first response within 4 business hours.
Severity 3 (question or minor issue): first response within 2 business days.

Enterprise customers on the Premium plan receive a named technical account manager. Customers on the Standard plan use the shared support queue.

Service credits: if monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the customer receives a 10% credit on that month's platform fee. Below 99.0%, the credit is 25%.
